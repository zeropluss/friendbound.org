Permissions Policy

Understanding how we use permissions and data access rights

Our Approach to Permissions

At Friendbound.org, we believe in transparency about how we use permissions and access rights to provide our mental health support services. This policy explains what permissions we request and why they're necessary.

Browser Permissions

Essential Permissions:

  • Storage Access: To save your preferences and login sessions
  • Network Access: To connect you with our support community
  • JavaScript: To provide interactive features and real-time support

Optional Permissions:

  • Notifications: To alert you about important community updates (requires your consent)
  • Location: To help you find local mental health resources (optional)
  • Camera/Microphone: For video support sessions (only when you choose to use them)

Data Access Rights

What We Access:

  • Account Information: Username, email, and profile details you provide
  • Community Content: Posts and messages you share in support groups
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our platform to improve services
  • Technical Data: Browser type, device information for compatibility

What We Don't Access:

  • Private Messages: Direct messages between users (encrypted)
  • Personal Files: Any files you don't explicitly upload
  • External Data: Information from other websites or services
  • Device Data: Photos, contacts, or other personal device information

Third-Party Permissions

We may use trusted third-party services to enhance our platform:

  • Analytics: To understand how our services are used and improve them
  • Security: To protect against fraud and ensure community safety
  • Support Tools: To provide better customer service
  • Payment Processing: For donations or premium features (if applicable)

Permission Management

Your Control:

  • Revoke Permissions: You can withdraw consent for optional permissions at any time
  • Account Settings: Manage your privacy preferences in your account settings
  • Data Export: Request a copy of your data at any time
  • Account Deletion: Permanently delete your account and all associated data

Security Measures

We implement strict security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Access Controls: Limited staff access to user data
  • Regular Audits: Regular security assessments and updates
  • Compliance: Adherence to data protection regulations

Emergency Access

Limited Emergency Access:

In rare emergency situations where someone's safety is at immediate risk, we may access account information to:

  • Contact emergency services if needed
  • Provide information to law enforcement with proper legal authority
  • Protect the safety of our community members

This access is strictly limited and requires proper legal authorization.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this permissions policy as our services evolve. We will notify you of any significant changes and provide you with the opportunity to review and consent to new permissions.

Contact Us

If you have questions about our permissions policy or want to manage your permissions:

  • Email: privacy@friendbound.org
  • Support: Contact our support team through your account
  • Legal: For legal inquiries about permissions and data access

Ready to Join Our Community?

We're committed to protecting your privacy while providing the support you need. Join our community with confidence knowing your data is secure.
