How Crisis Text Line Works

Crisis Text Line provides free, 24/7 crisis support via text message. It's a safe, confidential way to get help when you're experiencing a mental health crisis.

1

Text HOME to 741741

Send a text message to start a conversation with a trained crisis counselor.

2

Connect with a Counselor

You'll be matched with a trained volunteer who will listen and provide support.

3

Get Help & Resources

Receive immediate support and referrals to local resources if needed.

What to Expect

  • Immediate Response: You'll receive a response within minutes
  • Confidential: Your conversation is private and secure
  • Non-Judgmental: Counselors provide compassionate, understanding support
  • Professional: All counselors are trained volunteers
  • Free: No cost for the service (standard text rates may apply)

When to Use Crisis Text Line

Crisis Text Line is available for any crisis situation, including:

  • Thoughts of suicide or self-harm
  • Overwhelming anxiety or panic attacks
  • Depression or hopelessness
  • Relationship problems
  • Substance abuse concerns
  • Any emotional crisis where you need immediate support

Safety & Privacy

Your safety and privacy are our top priorities:

  • Confidentiality: Your conversations are private
  • Safety First: Counselors may contact emergency services if there's immediate danger
  • No Pressure: You can end the conversation at any time
  • Professional Standards: All counselors follow strict guidelines

⚠️ Emergency Situations

If you or someone else is in immediate danger:

  • Call 911 for emergency services
  • Go to the nearest emergency room
  • Call the National Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 988
