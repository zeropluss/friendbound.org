Support from Someone Who Understands

Our peer crisis support program connects you with trained volunteers who have experienced similar mental health challenges. These peer supporters provide understanding, hope, and practical guidance based on their own recovery journeys.

🤝 Lived Experience

Our peer supporters have walked similar paths and understand the challenges you're facing. They offer genuine empathy and hope from personal experience.

🎓 Trained & Certified

All peer supporters complete comprehensive training in crisis intervention, active listening, and safety protocols before providing support.

🔒 Confidential & Safe

All conversations are confidential and secure. Your privacy and safety are our top priorities.

⏰ Available Daily

Peer support is available daily during designated hours. In crisis situations, we can connect you with immediate professional help.

How Peer Crisis Support Works

1

Request Support

Fill out a brief form describing your situation and what type of support you're looking for.

2

Get Matched

We'll match you with a peer supporter who has relevant experience and training for your specific needs.

3

Connect & Share

Connect via chat, video call, or phone to share your experiences and receive support.

4

Ongoing Support

Continue connecting with your peer supporter or explore other resources as needed.

What Peer Supporters Can Help With

  • Emotional Support: Listening and providing understanding during difficult times
  • Hope & Encouragement: Sharing recovery stories and positive outcomes
  • Practical Guidance: Tips and strategies that worked for them
  • Resource Connection: Helping you find professional help and other resources
  • Crisis Intervention: Recognizing when professional help is needed
  • Recovery Planning: Supporting you in creating a plan for your mental health journey

Safety & Professional Oversight

While peer support is powerful, we maintain strict safety protocols:

  • Professional Supervision: All peer supporters work under the guidance of licensed mental health professionals
  • Crisis Protocols: Clear procedures for handling crisis situations and connecting with emergency services
  • Regular Training: Ongoing education and skill development for all peer supporters
  • Quality Assurance: Regular review and feedback to ensure high-quality support

⚠️ Important Note

Peer support is not a substitute for professional mental health care. If you're in crisis or experiencing thoughts of self-harm, please contact emergency services immediately:

  • Emergency: Call 911
  • Suicide Prevention: Call 988
  • Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741

Become a Peer Supporter

If you have lived experience with mental health challenges and want to help others, consider becoming a peer supporter. We provide comprehensive training and ongoing support.

  • Complete our peer support training program
  • Receive ongoing supervision and support
  • Make a meaningful difference in others' lives
  • Join a community of dedicated volunteers
