Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data protection are our top priorities

Your Privacy Matters

At Friendbound.org, we understand that privacy is especially important when discussing mental health. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide:

  • Account information (username, email address)
  • Profile information (optional personal details)
  • Community posts and messages
  • Support group participation
  • Feedback and communications with us

Automatically Collected Information:

  • Device information (browser type, operating system)
  • Usage data (pages visited, time spent)
  • IP address and location data
  • Cookies and similar technologies

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide Services: Operate our support groups and community features
  • Safety: Monitor for harmful content and ensure community safety
  • Improvement: Analyze usage to improve our services
  • Communication: Send important updates and notifications
  • Legal Compliance: Meet legal obligations and protect rights

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Secure hosting and infrastructure
  • Employee training on data protection

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal information
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities
  • Withdrawal: Withdraw consent where applicable

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@friendbound.org

Have Privacy Concerns?

We're committed to transparency and protecting your privacy. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
