What to Expect

📅 Response Time

  • Urgent matters: Within 2-4 hours
  • General inquiries: Within 24 hours
  • Complex issues: Within 48 hours with initial acknowledgment

🔒 Privacy & Confidentiality

  • All communications are kept confidential
  • We only share information with our safety team
  • Your identity is protected unless required by law
  • We follow strict data protection protocols

📋 Information We May Request

  • Specific details about the situation
  • Screenshots or evidence (if applicable)
  • Timeline of events
  • Any previous communications or reports
