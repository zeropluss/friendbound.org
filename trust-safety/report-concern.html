Report a Concern

Help us maintain a safe and supportive community by reporting any concerns

We Take Your Safety Seriously

At Friendbound.org, we are committed to maintaining a safe, supportive, and respectful environment for all our community members. If you encounter any concerning behavior or content, we encourage you to report it immediately.

What Should You Report?

Immediate Safety Concerns:

  • Self-harm threats: Any posts indicating someone may harm themselves
  • Harm to others: Threats of violence or harm to other people
  • Child safety: Any content involving minors inappropriately
  • Illegal activity: Posts promoting illegal activities

Community Guidelines Violations:

  • Harassment: Bullying, intimidation, or targeted abuse
  • Inappropriate content: Offensive, graphic, or inappropriate material
  • Spam: Unwanted commercial content or repetitive posts
  • False information: Misleading medical advice or harmful misinformation
  • Privacy violations: Sharing others' personal information without consent

How to Report

Report Form

Optional - we'll only contact you if we need more information

What Happens Next?

1

Immediate Review

Our safety team reviews all reports within 24 hours, with urgent concerns addressed immediately.

2

Investigation

We investigate the reported content and gather additional context if needed.

3

Action Taken

Appropriate action is taken, which may include content removal, user warnings, or account suspension.

4

Follow-up

If you provided contact information, we may reach out for additional details or to inform you of actions taken.

Emergency Situations

If someone is in immediate danger:

  • Call 911 (US) or your local emergency number
  • Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 988 (US)
  • Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741 (US)
  • Emergency Services: Contact local emergency services immediately

Please report the concern here as well, but do not delay in contacting emergency services if someone's safety is at immediate risk.

Your Privacy

We take your privacy seriously. When you submit a report:

  • Your identity is kept confidential unless required by law
  • We only share information with our safety team
  • We do not disclose who made the report to the person being reported
  • Your report helps us maintain community safety

Need Immediate Help?

If you're experiencing a mental health crisis, please reach out to professional help immediately.
