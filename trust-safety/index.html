Community Guidelines

Creating a safe and supportive environment for everyone

Be Respectful & Kind

Treat all community members with respect, empathy, and kindness. Everyone's mental health journey is unique and valid.

Maintain Confidentiality

Respect the privacy of others. Do not share personal information or conversations outside of the community.

Provide Support, Not Advice

Share experiences and support, but avoid giving medical or professional advice. Encourage professional help when needed.

Report Concerns

If you see concerning behavior or content, report it immediately. Our moderation team will address all reports promptly.

Stay on Topic

Keep discussions focused on mental health support and related topics. Avoid off-topic conversations that may disrupt the community.

No Harmful Content

Do not post content that promotes self-harm, violence, or harmful behaviors. We prioritize the safety of all community members.

Need Help or Have Concerns?

Our safety team is here to help. Contact us immediately if you have any concerns about your safety or the safety of others.

Comprehensive Mental Health Community Safety and Trust Building

At Friendbound.org, we understand that trust and safety are the foundation of any effective mental health support community. Our comprehensive safety framework is designed to protect every member while fostering an environment where genuine healing and connection can flourish.

Multi-Layered Safety Architecture

Our safety system operates on multiple levels to ensure comprehensive protection for all community members. From initial registration and verification to ongoing monitoring and crisis intervention, every aspect of our platform is designed with safety as the primary consideration.

Professional Oversight and Moderation

All our support groups and community spaces are actively moderated by trained mental health professionals and experienced community moderators. These professionals understand both the clinical aspects of mental health care and the unique dynamics of online support communities.

Advanced Technology for Safety

We employ cutting-edge technology to monitor community interactions and identify potential safety concerns. Our AI-powered systems work alongside human moderators to ensure that harmful content is detected and addressed quickly and effectively.

Privacy by Design

Privacy is not an afterthought at Friendbound.org - it's built into every aspect of our platform. We follow privacy-by-design principles, ensuring that user data is protected from the ground up through robust encryption, minimal data collection, and transparent data practices.

Crisis Intervention and Emergency Response

We recognize that mental health crises can occur at any time, which is why we maintain 24/7 crisis intervention capabilities. Our trained crisis responders are available around the clock to provide immediate support and connect individuals with appropriate emergency services when needed.

Community Guidelines and Behavioral Standards

Our community guidelines are designed to create a supportive, respectful environment where all members feel safe to share their experiences and seek support. These guidelines are enforced consistently and fairly, with clear processes for reporting violations and appealing decisions.

Transparency and Accountability

We believe in transparency and accountability in all our safety practices. We regularly share information about our safety measures, respond to community concerns, and continuously improve our systems based on feedback and emerging best practices.

Education and Prevention

We believe that education is a key component of safety. We provide regular training to our moderators, educate community members about safety practices, and work to prevent safety issues before they occur through proactive measures and clear communication.

Partnerships with Mental Health Professionals

We maintain partnerships with mental health organizations and professionals to ensure that our safety practices align with current best practices in mental health care. These partnerships help us stay current with emerging safety concerns and effective intervention strategies.

Continuous Improvement and Adaptation

Our safety systems are continuously evolving to address new challenges and incorporate the latest research and best practices. We regularly review and update our safety measures based on community feedback, emerging threats, and advances in technology and mental health care.

Your Safety is Our Priority

Every decision we make, every feature we develop, and every policy we implement is guided by our commitment to your safety and well-being. We believe that only in a truly safe environment can meaningful healing and connection occur, and we're dedicated to maintaining that environment for all our community members.
