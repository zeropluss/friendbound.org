Community Guidelines

Creating a safe, supportive environment for everyone

Our Commitment to Safety

At Friendbound.org, we're committed to creating a safe, supportive environment where everyone can share their mental health journey without fear of judgment or harm.

Core Values

  • Compassion: Treat everyone with kindness and understanding
  • Respect: Honor different experiences and perspectives
  • Safety: Protect the well-being of all community members
  • Support: Offer encouragement and help to those in need
  • Privacy: Respect personal boundaries and confidentiality

What We Encourage

  • Sharing personal experiences and feelings
  • Offering supportive and encouraging messages
  • Asking questions about mental health topics
  • Sharing helpful resources and coping strategies
  • Celebrating progress and recovery milestones
  • Reporting concerning behavior to moderators

What We Don't Allow

Prohibited Content:

  • Harmful advice: Medical advice, treatment recommendations, or crisis management
  • Discrimination: Hate speech, bullying, or targeting based on identity
  • Self-harm promotion: Content that encourages or glorifies self-harm
  • Spam: Commercial content, advertising, or irrelevant links
  • Personal attacks: Insults, threats, or harassment of other members
  • Misinformation: False claims about mental health or treatments
  • Privacy violations: Sharing others' personal information

Crisis Situations

If you or someone else is in crisis:

  • Call 911 for immediate emergency assistance
  • National Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 988 or 1-800-273-8255
  • Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
  • Emergency Room: Go to your nearest hospital

Our community is not a substitute for professional crisis intervention.

Ready to Join Our Community?

By participating in our community, you agree to follow these guidelines and help create a supportive environment for everyone.
