Make a Difference

Join our mission to provide accessible, safe, and effective mental health support to people around the world. We're looking for passionate individuals who want to make a real impact in mental health care.

Community Moderator

Full-time

Help maintain safe, supportive environments in our online support groups. Work with our community to ensure everyone feels heard and supported.

Requirements:

  • Experience in mental health support or community moderation
  • Strong communication and conflict resolution skills
  • Ability to work flexible hours including evenings and weekends
  • Training in crisis intervention (we provide training)
Apply Now

Software Engineer

Full-time

Build and maintain the technology that powers our mental health support platform. Help create safe, accessible, and user-friendly experiences.

Requirements:

  • Experience with modern web technologies (React, Node.js, etc.)
  • Understanding of security best practices
  • Experience with accessibility standards
  • Passion for mental health and community building
Apply Now

Mental Health Content Specialist

Part-time

Create educational content and resources that help our community members understand and manage their mental health.

Requirements:

  • Background in mental health, psychology, or related field
  • Strong writing and communication skills
  • Ability to translate complex topics into accessible content
  • Understanding of evidence-based mental health practices

Why Work With Us?

Meaningful Impact

Every day, you'll help people find support, hope, and connection in their mental health journey.

Professional Growth

Continuous learning opportunities and career development in the mental health technology space.

Flexible Work

Remote work options and flexible schedules to support your work-life balance.

Supportive Team

Work with passionate professionals who care about mental health and community building.

Our Values

We look for team members who share our core values:

  • Compassion: Genuine care for the well-being of others
  • Innovation: Creative thinking to solve complex challenges
  • Safety: Commitment to protecting our community members
  • Accessibility: Making mental health support available to everyone
  • Evidence: Basing our work on scientific research and best practices

Application Process

  1. Submit Application: Send your resume and cover letter to careers@friendbound.org
  2. Initial Review: We'll review your application and reach out within 1-2 weeks
  3. Interview Process: Multiple rounds including skills assessment and team interviews
  4. Reference Check: We'll contact your references to learn more about your work
  5. Offer & Onboarding: Welcome to the team!
