About Our PTSD Support Group

Our PTSD support group is specifically designed for trauma survivors, providing a trauma-informed environment where individuals can safely share their experiences and work toward healing. With over 1,200 members, our community understands the unique challenges of living with PTSD.

What You'll Find Here

  • Trauma-Informed Care: Our community operates with deep understanding of trauma's impact
  • Safe Environment: A space where triggers are respected and safety is prioritized
  • Healing Focus: Support for your journey toward recovery and healing
  • Professional Guidance: Access to trauma specialists and evidence-based resources
  • Peer Support: Connection with others who understand trauma's effects
  • 24/7 Availability: Support available whenever you need it

Types of Trauma We Support

Our community supports individuals dealing with various types of trauma:

  • Combat trauma (military service)
  • Sexual assault and abuse
  • Physical assault and violence
  • Accidents and injuries
  • Natural disasters
  • Medical trauma
  • Childhood trauma
  • Witnessing violence
  • Complex trauma

Our Trauma-Informed Approach

We understand that trauma affects everyone differently, and there's no "right way" to heal. Our community respects each individual's unique journey and provides support without judgment or pressure.

Safety and Triggers

We prioritize safety and are mindful of potential triggers. Our community guidelines include:

  • Content warnings for potentially triggering discussions
  • Respect for individual boundaries and comfort levels
  • No pressure to share more than you're comfortable with
  • Support for managing triggers and flashbacks
  • Professional crisis support when needed

Community Guidelines

To maintain a safe and supportive environment:

  • Always use content warnings for potentially triggering content
  • Respect each person's healing journey and pace
  • Share experiences without giving unsolicited advice
  • Maintain confidentiality and privacy
  • Seek professional help for crisis situations

Professional Moderation

Our group is moderated by trained trauma specialists and mental health professionals who ensure that all conversations remain safe and supportive. They're available to provide guidance and address concerns when needed.
